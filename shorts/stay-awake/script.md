# Stay awake for $100,000

A "last one awake wins" challenge where the cameras show everyone, even the host, slipped into
microsleeps with their eyes open, so nobody wins.

- Format: vertical Short, 2D cut-out cartoon
- Narration: 141 words, ~56 s at 150 wpm
- Hook formula: The Statistic (hookscore 70, WORKABLE)
- Voice-over only: [voiceover.txt](voiceover.txt)

## Hook options

| Score | Hook |
|---|---|
| 70 | Stay awake longer than everyone in this room and you win $100,000. But your brain will lose before you do. |
| 61 | You can fall asleep for 10 seconds with your eyes wide open, and that is how all three of these guys lost $100,000. |

## Sources

- Randy Gardner, 264 hours awake, January 1964; Guinness stopped tracking sleep-deprivation
  records in 1996: https://www.npr.org/2024/01/28/1227217274/sleep-deprivation-record
- Microsleeps last a few seconds, can happen with eyes open, often unnoticed:
  https://www.sleepfoundation.org/how-sleep-works/microsleep
- Microsleeps after sleep restriction (eye-tracking study): https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23998288/

Everything the characters do is cartoon fiction; the only real-world claims are the ones above.
